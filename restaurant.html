How it works

Order a few things. Pass them round.

Filipino meals are a table full of dishes, not a sequence of courses. Start with small plates, add a couple of mains to share, and leave room for something purple.

Small plates

Lumpia, crispy squid, pork belly bites, smoked aubergine salad. Three or four between two to start.

Sharing mains

Adobo, sisig, kare-kare, lechon-style pork, whole fish. Big, slow, made for the middle of the table.

Desserts

Tsunami cheesecake, ube halo-halo, leche flan. Nobody leaves without one.

The bar

Cocktails with a Filipino accent.

The Ube Martini leads, but it's not alone: a papaya tita margarita, calamansi sours, Filipino rum and a wine list that travels from Europe to the New World. Bar seats are walk-in; come for one before dinner or stay for three.
