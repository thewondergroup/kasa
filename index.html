Purple yam · Filipino soul

The Home of Ube

Ube (say “oo-beh”) is a violet yam from the Philippines with a flavour somewhere between vanilla, pistachio and white chocolate. Every Filipino childhood has it: in halo-halo, in jam, in birthday cake. At Kasa & Kin it runs through the whole day — and it's the one ingredient our founder says she can't live without.

The Ube Martini

Vodka, coconut, cranberry and ube — the drink Soho talks about. Sweet, creamy, earthy and very, very purple.

Our story

South East Asia's best-kept secret, served in Soho.

Raising the profile of Filipino food in London has always been our mission. We're a family business, and in 2021 we brought Filipino cooking to Poland Street: a restaurant, bakery and bar under one roof in London's most dynamic food district.

“Filipino cuisine can finally take its rightful place among the great cuisines of Asia.”

Rowena Romulo, co-founder
